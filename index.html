How It Works

The Legal Attack Surface Your Security Stack Can't See.

Every tool in your stack points inward. Darrow looks from the outside — the same vantage point regulators and plaintiff firms already use — and turns what they'd find into intelligence you can act on first. No agents to deploy. No data to share.

01

Submit One Domain

Enter a public domain name. That's the entire setup — no integration, no credentials, no internal access. Deploy in minutes, not months.

02

Agents Scan Continuously

Autonomous AI agents map your external footprint — trackers, pixels, consent flows, data-sharing — exactly as it appears to the outside world, and keep watching as it changes.

03

Exposure Gets Scored

Every finding is severity-scored, evidence-backed, and mapped to the specific law, jurisdiction, and enforcement precedent that makes it a risk — with the dollar exposure quantified.

04

You Act Before It Escalates

Get remediation steps prioritized by team and urgency — a board-ready view of exposure you can close before it becomes an investigation, a demand letter, or a class action.
